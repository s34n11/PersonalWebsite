Research Interests

My academic interests lie broadly at the intersection of cognitive science, mathematics, and artificial intelligence. I am particularly interested in understanding the mechanisms underlying intelligent behaviour, both in biological and artificial systems.

01

Mechanistic Interpretability & AI Alignment

I am interested in understanding how the internal representations of large language models contribute to their behaviour, and whether developing greater insight into these mechanisms can help address problems in AI safety and alignment.

02

Computational Cognitive Science

I am interested in how computational models can be used to better understand human cognition, particularly learning, reasoning, and decision-making. More broadly, I am fascinated by the relationship between the computational and neural mechanisms underlying intelligent behaviour.

03

Mathematical Foundations of Intelligence

I am interested in the mathematical foundations of machine learning, particularly how ideas from linear algebra, probability, and statistics can be used to understand the structure and behaviour of learning systems.
