Coursework

Selected coursework completed at the University of Toronto across mathematics, statistics, computer science, cognitive science, and psychology.

Mathematics

MAT224H1

Linear Algebra II

A deeper study of vector spaces and linear operators, including change of basis, inner-product spaces, diagonalization, the spectral theorem, and Jordan form.

MAT235Y1

Multivariable & Vector Calculus

Multivariable differential and integral calculus, including partial derivatives, multiple integrals, line and surface integrals, and core vector-calculus ideas.

MAT246H1

Concepts in Abstract Mathematics

An introduction to mathematical proof and abstraction, with topics including number theory, modular arithmetic, infinite sets, and rigorous proof techniques.

Statistics

STA220H1

The Practice of Statistics I

An introduction to statistical reasoning, exploratory data analysis, experimental design, probability, estimation, and hypothesis testing.

STA255H1

Statistical Theory

Mathematical foundations of probability and statistics, including probability distributions, expectation, sampling distributions, estimation, hypothesis testing, and linear models.

STA302H1

Methods of Data Analysis I

Regression-based data analysis covering simple and multiple regression, statistical inference, diagnostics, interactions, categorical predictors, and model selection.

Computer Science

CSC236H1

Introduction to the Theory of Computation

Mathematical reasoning for computer science, including induction, recursive algorithms, correctness proofs, recurrence relations, automata, and formal languages.

CSC263H1

Data Structures and Analysis

Analysis and implementation of efficient data structures and algorithms, including complexity analysis, randomized algorithms, trees, hashing, graphs, and disjoint-set structures.

CSC209H1

Software Tools and Systems Programming

Systems programming in C and Unix, including processes, memory management, file I/O, signals, pipes, sockets, and command-line development tools.

Cognitive Science & Psychology

COG250Y1

Introduction to Cognitive Science

An interdisciplinary study of the mind drawing from psychology, philosophy, linguistics, neuroscience, and computer science.

PSY270H1

Introduction to Cognitive Psychology

Research and theory on the cognitive and neural architecture of attention, memory, language, thinking, and reasoning.

PSY290H1

Behavioural Neuroscience

The biological basis of behaviour, including functional neuroanatomy, neural signalling, sensory and motor systems, motivation, hormones, and emotion.

COG260H1

Data, Computation, and the Mind

Computational approaches to studying cognition, with an emphasis on formal models, data-driven analysis, and the use of computational methods to investigate cognitive processes.
